Get the app

On your phone in ten seconds.

Roost installs straight from your browser, no app store needed. Native iPhone and Android apps with voice calls are next.

iPhone

  1. Open Roost in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open Roost in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app.
Questions
Is Roost free?

Yes. Everything is free during the beta. Roost+ and Server Boost add AI extras and bigger limits later, but chatting, servers, hangouts and games stay free.

How do I invite friends?

Open your server's menu and tap Invite people. Send them the code; they tap the compass, then "Have a code?".

Are there voice calls?

Hangouts show who's in the room, with a shared soundboard and emotes. Real voice and video arrive with the native phone and desktop apps.

What can the AI do?

@mention Roost AI to ask questions or settle debates, tap Catch-up to summarise a busy channel, translate messages, write trivia questions and help mods with auto-moderation.

What about privacy?

No ads and no selling your data. Block anyone in one tap, and server owners control who can join with invite codes and bans. Don't share passwords or bank details in any chat app, Roost included.
