What's different

Everything Discord does. Plus the part where people actually show up.

Plans that open rooms

Type /tonight 9pm. Friends tap In, Maybe or Out. At 9 the voice room opens and pings everyone who's in.

Catch-up

Missed 400 messages? Get the five lines that matter, including anything that mentions you.

Hangout rooms

Always-on voice rooms you can drop into. Status shows what people are doing, so you know when to join.

1080p screen share

Stream games and films at 60fps to the whole room, with no lag on the free plan for up to 10 people.

Works with Discord

Add the Tonight bot to your existing server and keep everyone where they are until they're ready to move.

Private by default

No ads and no selling your data. DMs are end-to-end encrypted. Server owners can export everything.
