How it works

Three steps from “anyone?” to everyone.

Step 1

Call it

Pick a time and what you're doing. Type 9pm valorant and Tonight works out the rest.

Step 2

Everyone taps in

Friends get one notification with three buttons: In, Maybe, Out. No scrolling, no reading back.

Step 3

The room opens

At the set time the voice room goes live and pings everyone who said yes. Maybes get a nudge 15 minutes before.

Questions
Do my friends need to leave Discord?

No. Start with the Discord bot. It handles plans and RSVPs inside your existing server. The full app is there when you want hangout rooms and catch-up.

Is it only for gaming?

No. People use it for movie nights, study sessions, five-a-side and anything else where the hard part is getting everyone there at the same time.

How does catch-up handle my messages?

Summaries are made only for servers you're in, only from messages you can already see, and they aren't used to train models. Server owners can turn it off.

What happens if only one person says yes?

You choose a minimum. If not enough people are in 15 minutes before, Tonight asks whether to keep it, move it or call it off.

When does it launch?

The Discord bot opens to the waitlist first, followed by the desktop and mobile apps.
